AgentOS 1.0.2

- The installer boots as a (virtual) CD on Unraid and other hosts whose CD drive is slow or briefly unreadable at boot. 1.0.1 could stop in emergency mode ("Failed to start systemd-volatile-root.service ... the root account is locked"); the installer now keeps looking for its CD for up to 10 minutes and prints why it is waiting.
- The installer is also offered as one .iso file at https://dl.currentbits.net/agentos/index.html; its SHA-256 is now in this release's SHA256SUMS.
- Weekly development builds are marked as pre-releases, so "latest release" always means the newest real release.
